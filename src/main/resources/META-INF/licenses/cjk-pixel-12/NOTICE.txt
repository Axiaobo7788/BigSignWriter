The generated font data is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
It retains regional BDF glyph pixels without resampling, except for documented
derivative corrections in tools/cjk-font-generator/corrections/. The default
zh_hans U+9053 (道) correction expands the inner eye to show two distinct strokes;
the left-hand radical, 12x12 cell, width and character coverage are unchanged.
These corrected glyphs are also distributed under OFL-1.1. ASCII, PUA glyphs
and oversized vertical Kana ligatures are excluded from the bundled provider.
The derivative family name is CJK Pixel 12, distinct from the upstream Fusion Pixel name.
